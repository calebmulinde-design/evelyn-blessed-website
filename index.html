ABOUT THE ARTIST

Welcome to the official home of Evelyn Blessed. She is a singer and songwriter born in Uganda , Africa. She sets out to inspire the world with her music one step at a time.

Living in the United States at the moment, she still releases beautiful music and continues to inspire the next generation of singers. She looks forward to great things in the life that God has blessed her with in the states.
